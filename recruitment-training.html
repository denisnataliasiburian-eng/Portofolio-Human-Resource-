Professional Recruitment

Online Workshop · HR Talk Indonesia

Professional recruitment training covering recruitment strategies, candidate assessment, interviewing, and approaches for evaluating candidates throughout the recruitment process.

TRAINING OVERVIEW

Understanding recruitment beyond candidate selection.

01

Recruitment Strategy

Recruitment methods, internal and external recruitment, diversity recruitment, and hiring strategies.

02

Candidate Assessment

Psychological testing, digital assessment, and approaches for evaluating candidates.

03

Interview

Interview structures, BEI, STAR approach, and situational interview questions.

KEY TOPICS

Professional recruitment practices.

The workshop covered several aspects of professional recruitment, from recruitment methods to candidate interviewing and assessment.

  • Recruitment and selection framework
  • Internal and external recruitment
  • Diversity recruitment
  • Recruitment and hiring strategies
  • Psychological testing and digital assessment
  • Types of interview
  • Successful interview structure
  • Behavioral Event Interview (BEI)
  • STAR approach
  • Situational queries
  • Interviewing job hoppers
  • Collaboration and adaptability

LEARNING FOCUS

Recruitment, assessment, and interviewing

The training provided a broader understanding of recruitment as a process that involves sourcing, assessment, interviewing, and hiring decisions. Particular attention was given to structured interviewing techniques and candidate evaluation.

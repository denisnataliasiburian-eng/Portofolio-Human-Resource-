PKM-PIMNAS Research Project

Psychological Interventions for Generation Alpha Elementary School Students Who Have Experienced Sexual Violence in Bekasi City

Research Psychology Trauma-Informed Care PIMNAS

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Research with a people-centered approach.

This research project focused on psychological interventions for Generation Alpha elementary school students who experienced sexual violence in Bekasi City. The project applied a Trauma-Informed Care approach to support the development of a safer and more responsive school environment.

The project was developed through PKM-RSH and successfully advanced to the National Student Scientific Week (PIMNAS) 2025.
